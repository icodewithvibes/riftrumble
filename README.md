# Rift Rumble
An original 2D platform fighter in the browser: https://icodewithvibes.github.io/riftrumble/

Six champions from six torn-apart worlds, a story mode with an ending for each fighter, local versus for up to 4 players (keyboard or controllers), and online play with rollback netcode.

Online: Online > Create Room and share the 4-letter code; friends open the same link, Online > Join Room. Or use Quick Match. Peer-to-peer, no sign-in.

Saves (settings, story progress) stay in your browser.
